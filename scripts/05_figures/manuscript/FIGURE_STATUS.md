## Status definitions

- **Complete:** documented inputs, portable runner, output, and session record.
- **Partly complete:** method is known, but a portable input or assembly remains.
- **Historical only:** provenance copy; not intended to be run.

## Figures 3 and 4 — Braak analyses

**Complete.** The portable workflow uses the archived four-study workbook,
validates numeric or Roman Braak stages, prepares ComBat-corrected expression
once, and produces both figures. Run both with:

```r
source("scripts/05_figures/manuscript/shared_braak/run_figures3_and_4.R")
```

All 201 staged participants are included because an AD-only subset has no
low-stage observations in three regions. Significance asterisks are restricted
to the overall and regional waterfall plots; the forest plot has none.

## Figure 5 — external validation

**Complete.** Public GSE125583 and controlled-access MSBB are evaluated with
fixed elastic-net settings and donor-grouped five-fold cross-validation. Fold
assignments are written with predictions and Figure 5 computes fold AUCs from
those assignments. Restricted MSBB source data remain outside the repository
and are supplied through `MSBB_EXPR` and `MSBB_META`.
